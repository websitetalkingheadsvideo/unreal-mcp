## Visual recipe

- **Sheet:** off-white with visible grain; no color fills except symbolic marks.
- **Structure:** pure black linework; exterior walls thickest; high binary contrast.
- **Typography:** stylized serif shop title; technical ALL CAPS for PARTY WALL, STREET, REAR ACCESS.
- **Symbols:** occult glyphs (ankh, ritual marks) as plan furniture icons; bells, shelves, counters as simple line icons.
- **Mood:** shadowed archival scout of a mundane facade hiding arcane inventory.
