## Visual recipe

- **Sheet:** cream/off-white with uniform paper grain; double-line rectangular border.
- **Structure:** deep navy ink; heavy exterior walls with dark fill; thin interior detail lines.
- **Grid:** faint square grid under plan (optional, subtle).
- **Typography:** serif ALL CAPS titles; room names as `ROOM NAME (Function)`; formal title block at top.
- **Symbols:** ritual floor patterns as line art only; stair to basement/sanctum labeled; garden/grounds sketched softer than interior.
